About us

A casual, cozy bar and grill in Whitby.

Good food in a relaxed room

Michael Kelly's Bar & Grill is a neighbourhood spot on Winchester Road East in Whitby. The atmosphere is casual and cozy, and it works for a quick bite on your own, a family meal or a get-together with a group.

The kitchen serves lunch and dinner, with dessert, coffee, beer and wine as well. You can dine in with table service, sit outside, or take your meal to go.

Families are welcome, with a kids' menu and high chairs available. Parking is free, and the entrance, parking lot and washroom are wheelchair accessible.

Our story

Michael Kelly's describes itself as family owned and operated, with a story that goes back to 1972, when Angelo started making pizza in Scarborough, Ontario. Many people in the area remember eating that pizza when they were kids.

That recipe is still part of the menu today. The pizza menu credits Angelo's original recipe, started in 1972, and says the secret is in the crust.

Today you'll find us in Brooklin, a community in the northern part of Whitby, at 93 Winchester Road East. It is sometimes listed as Michael Kelly's Brooklin. Customers have also mentioned that the restaurant is under new management and has been refreshed.

The menu today

The menu mixes classic pub fare with global-inspired dishes. You'll find pizza, wings, poutines, wraps, salads, pastas and Asian Delights, plus a kids menu and a weekend and holiday breakfast.
